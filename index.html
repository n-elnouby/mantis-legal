Privacy Policy

MANTIS · Last updated: March 2026

Overview

MANTIS is a personal health and wellness tracking application developed by Noubyte. Your privacy is fundamental to how we built MANTIS. The app is designed with a local-first architecture — your data stays on your devices.

Data we collect

MANTIS collects the following types of data, all of which remain on your device or in your personal iCloud account:

  • Health & fitness data — read from Apple Health with your explicit permission, including heart rate, HRV, sleep, workouts, steps, respiratory rate, and related metrics.
  • Self-reported data — symptom logs, mood ratings, custom routines, and journal entries you create within the app.
  • Nutrition data — meal logs and macronutrient data you enter or import.
  • Profile information — your name and date of birth, used to personalise the experience.

How data is stored

All data is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, your data syncs privately to your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. We do not operate servers that store your data. We cannot access, view, or retrieve your health information.

Third-party services

MANTIS uses two public nutrition databases for food lookups — the USDA FoodData Central API and Open Food Facts. When you search for a food, your search query is sent to these services. No personal or health data is included in these requests. No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included in the app.

Apple Health

You can modify Health permissions at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MANTIS. The app also writes workout data back to Apple Health when you complete a workout.

Scientific sources & methods

MANTIS is a wellness tool, not a medical device. Its estimates and suggestions are based on published research and public data sources. The same list is available inside the app under Settings → Sources & Methods, with a link next to the calculations it explains. Sources are provided for transparency and education, not as medical advice.

  • Protein target (1.6–2.2 g/kg) — Morton RW, et al. Br J Sports Med. 2018;52(6):376–384. source

  • Vigorous intermittent lifestyle activity (VILPA) — Stamatakis E, et al. Nat Med. 2022;28(12):2521–2529. source

Data deletion

You can delete all MANTIS data at any time from Settings → Erase MANTIS database. Apple Health data is not affected.

Health Disclaimer

MANTIS is a personal wellness tool designed to help you explore patterns in your health data. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

The insights, correlations, recommendations, and biological-age estimates provided by MANTIS are based on statistical analysis of your self-reported data and Apple Health metrics. They should not be interpreted as medical advice.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making changes to your diet, exercise routine, medication, or any aspect of your health management.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency services immediately.
